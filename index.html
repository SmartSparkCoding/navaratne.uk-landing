Small things I’ve built, all on one domain.

I’m a student who makes websites and tools for school, for Hack Club, and for problems that were annoying me enough to fix. Everything below is public. Pick something and have a look.

01 Start here

  • My personal site jacob.navaratne.uk Who I am, what I’m working on, and the projects I’d actually show someone.
  • Huddle Overlay Download overlay.navaratne.uk Your Slack huddle thread in a small floating window, on top of everything else. macOS and Windows, runs locally.
  • DrawingsGone Download drawingsgone.navaratne.uk Share your screen without the pen marks on it. Keeps an annotation layer out of what other people can see.

02 School & study

  • Revision Sheet Line revision.navaratne.uk Topic-by-topic revision sheets, laid out one after another so nothing gets lost.
  • Commendation Tracker rewards.navaratne.uk Keeps a running count of school commendations so I stop trying to remember them.
  • Edu Apps edu.navaratne.uk A small collection of study tools and lesson helpers, in one place.
  • An Inspector Calls quotes aic.navaratne.uk Searchable quotes from the play, sorted by act and character, for essays and last-minute revision.
  • Cluedo Sheets cluedo.navaratne.uk Old-school detective notes: cross off suspects, weapons and rooms. Saves in your browser, exports to a file.
  • Christmas Fair 2026 pfa.navaratne.uk The Ashford School PFA fair: stalls, Santa’s Grotto and rides, Sunday 29 November at Ashford Prep School.
